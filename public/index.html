About this Tool

Guest Guide Intelligence Scanner v4 — Intelligence Feed, Article Writer, Post Analytics, Content Calendar.

The scanner queries live web sources on every run across 80+ sources including EU institutions, national tourism bodies, consumer magazines, market intelligence firms, Nordic tourism bodies, rural and LEADER programme sources, and advocacy networks. The three-step Article Writer produces LinkedIn-optimised articles at Year-11 reading level, 1,000–1,200 words, with photorealistic image prompts, LinkedIn précis, and one-click .docx export for direct blog upload.

Post Analytics tracks all 12 posts from Sep 2025, with full demographic breakdown per post. Add new posts via the form at the bottom of the Analytics tab. Articles can be saved and downloaded as formatted .docx files directly from the Article Writer.
