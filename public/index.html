About This Platform

Guest Guide Content Intelligence is a private editorial platform for Walt Cudlip / Guest Guide Interactive. It scans live European tourism intelligence sources, saves results permanently in Vercel KV storage, and manages the full article pipeline from signal to publication to analytics.

Every scan creates a dated record that is never overwritten. Signals are filtered to the last 14 days to match a twice-weekly publishing schedule and ensure genuine news currency.
